I'm Michael, also known as Mindwrapper. I work in connectivity and networking, and every week I spend a lot of time working with AI: building tools, automating my notes, and figuring out where it helps and where it doesn't. This blog shows the things I'm wrapping my mind around.

## Who writes this

**Most posts here are not written by me.** They are written by AI, based on real work sessions from that week. My part:

- **I choose.** Each week we pick two topics from what actually happened, and I make the final call.
- **I review.** Nothing goes online until I've read it, checked it, and approved it.
- **I'm accountable.** If something here is wrong, that's on me, not on the tool.

You can see this on every post. AI posts carry *Written by AI · curated and reviewed by Mindwrapper*. Posts I write myself, usually the more personal ones, carry *Written by Mindwrapper*.

## Why

I don't have the time to write every week, but I do have a lot worth sharing. The idea to let AI write, and to be open about it rather than awkward, came from a discussion in the open source world about AI-generated contributions. The point that stuck with me: it doesn't matter much who or what wrote something. What matters is whether it's good, whether a human checked it, and whether it adds something. Writing has become cheap, so the review is where the value is.

## What you'll find in a post

- A short piece: the problem, what we did, and the insight.
- Where it makes sense, **the code on GitHub**.
- **A prompt to try it yourself.** Most people use AI anyway, so rather than copying my setup, you can hand the prompt to your own assistant and build your own version.

Every post is available in [[nl/over|Dutch]] and English.
