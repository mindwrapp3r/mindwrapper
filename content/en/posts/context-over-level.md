Last week I asked Claude to look at my conversations and give feedback on how I could improve my prompts. One of the suggestions was to state my knowledge level on the topic in the prompt.

The benefit is that the answer isn't generic, but pitched at my level.

Claude gave me a few examples of topics along with my knowledge level. If Claude already knows, why should I have to add it? So I asked Claude to build a prompt profile from my chats, with my level of expertise per domain. That profile is now sent along automatically with every chat.

The result: answers are tuned to what I already know, without me having to say it each time.
